## 4. TYPOGRAPHIC GEOMETRY MAP

1. **Oversized Display Headings (Serif / Display):**
   * **Style:** High-contrast vintage serif with rounded soft serifs and organic curves (Bookman / Caslon / Recife Display style).
   * **Scale:** 
     * Hero Title: `3.5rem` – `4.5rem` (`56px` – `72px`), line-height `1.1`.
     * Section Titles ("Choose your flavor", "Why people keep coming back", "Got questions?"): `2.75rem` – `3.5rem` (`44px` – `56px`).
     * Story Headline ("Our Story"): `4rem` – `5.5rem` (`64px` – `88px`), ultra-bold.
   * **Alignment:** Centered for Hero, Bento headers, Flavor header, Testimonials, and FAQ. Left-aligned for select story paragraphs.

2. **Section Eyebrow Badges (Pills):**
   * **Style:** Small caps / uppercase sans-serif enclosed in an oval pill container with border (`border: 1px solid currentColor`).
   * **Scale:** `0.75rem` – `0.875rem` (`12px` – `14px`), letter-spacing `0.1em`.
   * **Alignment:** Centered above section headlines ("BENEFITS", "OUR FLAVORS", "TESTIMONIALS", "FAQ").

3. **Card Titles & Subheadings:**
   * **Style:** Bold display serif or heavy rounded sans-serif.
   * **Scale:** `1.5rem` – `2.25rem` (`24px` – `36px`).

4. **Body Copy & Subtext:**
   * **Style:** Clean sans-serif or legibility serif (Inter / Outfit / Satoshi style).
   * **Scale:** `1rem` – `1.125rem` (`16px` – `18px`), line-height `1.5`.

5. **Oversized Footer Logotype:**
   * **Style:** Wall-to-wall stacked 3D striped brand typography spanning entire viewport width (`10vw`+ scale per letter).

## 5. IMAGE GEOMETRY MAP

1. **Studio Product Cutouts & 3D Pedestals:**
   * Products (drink cartons) are shot in studio setting on geometric wooden/colored pedestals with real shadows and surrounding ingredient props (chocolate blocks, caramel squares, fresh strawberries).
   * Products sit cleanly inside or slightly overlap their parent container boundaries.

2. **Rounded Bento Cards & Color Block Containers:**
   * All cards feature generous outer corner radiuses (`border-radius: 24px` to `32px`).
   * Color cards use solid saturated backgrounds (Mustard Yellow, Terracotta Red, Muted Brown, Coral Pink, Cyan Blue).

3. **Cutout Figure Overlaps (Founder Card):**
   * Portrait image of founder features subject background isolation (cutout head/shoulders) extending vertically *above* and *below* the yellow rectangular card container, breaking the flat container boundary.

4. **Circular Badges & Media Buttons:**
   * Floating circular badge ("PLAY VIDEO") with rotating curved text path SVG around a central icon.
   * Circular year timeline avatars (`border-radius: 50%`) with golden outline rings.

5. **Organic Wave Mask Dividers:**
   * Smooth, flowing horizontal wave SVG paths dividing major chromatic background sections (Hero pink to cream, cream to dark brown, dark brown to cream, cream to dark brown footer).

## 7. MOTION MAP (Behavior & Animation Authority)

1. **Smooth Scroll Engine:** Powered by **Lenis** (`lenis.css` / `lenis.js`) for fluid inertia-based page scrolling.
2. **Scroll-Triggered Reveals:** Elements use GSAP ScrollTrigger timeline animations (fade up, scale in, badge opacity reveal).
3. **Infinite Marquee Ticker:** Continuous CSS horizontal animation (`transform: translateX()`) for tag badges.
4. **Interactive Stagger / Pinned Flavor Cards:** Cards pin or stack gracefully during vertical scroll with smooth opacity/transform shifts.
5. **Rotating Badge Marquee:** 360-degree continuous rotation on the "PLAY VIDEO" circular badge text path.
6. **Accordion Toggles:** Smooth height transition (`max-height` / CSS grid row expand) with 45-degree rotation on plus `+` icons.
7. **Button Microinteractions:** Scale transform on hover, arrow icon sliding horizontally (`transform: translateX(4px)`).

## 8. RESPONSIVE MAP (Mobile Architecture)

1. **Desktop to Mobile Layout Adaptation:**
   * Asymmetric 2x2 Bento grid stacks vertically into a single-column card list on screens `< 768px`.
   * Staggered offset Flavor cards align into full-width stacked vertical cards.
   * Floating play button scales down (~80px) and positions below hero product group.
2. **Mobile Navigation:**
   * Sticky top header transforms into fixed bar with circular hamburger button opening full-screen overlay menu.
3. **Typography Scaling:**
   * Fluid typography scale using CSS `clamp()` or Webflow vw-calculated root font sizes (`font-size: calc(0.75rem + 0.83vw)`). Headings scale down from 64px desktop to 36px mobile while retaining visual weight.

## 9. TEN STRUCTURAL SIGNATURES (Invariant Architecture)

If all logos, copy, colors, and photos were stripped from this reference, these **10 invariant architectural rules** must remain intact to preserve visual fidelity:

1. **Top Floating Pill Navigation & Announcement Bar:** Fixed header container with rounded pill buttons and top banner notification.
2. **Organic Wave Dividers:** Smooth curved SVG section mask dividers separating contrasting background color blocks.
3. **Hero Composition with 3-Item Center Group & Floating Badge:** Centered display title, central 3-item product lineup, and an asymmetrical floating circular badge.
4. **Infinite Horizontal Pill Ticker:** Full-bleed horizontal marquee bar of rounded tag badges directly below the Hero section.
5. **Asymmetric Rounded Bento Grid:** Grid of cards with `border-radius: 24px+` alternating between solid color text cards and full photo cards.
6. **Staggered / Pinned Feature Cards:** Alternating left/right offset cards with embedded product pedestals.
7. **Pill Eyebrow Section Anchors:** Centered outline pill badges acting as section category tags ("BENEFITS", "OUR FLAVORS", etc.).
8. **Dramatic Light-to-Dark Atmospheric Shift:** High-contrast transition from bright/cream sections into a deep dark chocolate brown background for brand storytelling.
9. **Container-Breaking Cutout Overlaps:** Subject imagery (such as founder or key product cutouts) physically breaking container boundaries and overlapping text/card edges.
10. **Wall-to-Wall Oversized Graphic Footer:** Massive screen-width typographic footer anchor spanning 100% viewport width.
